# 01 — Product Overview: AI Business Operating System (AI-BOS)

**Tagline:** *Run Your Business. Live Your Life.*  
**Master Specification:** PRD v3.2 Final Master Release & FRD v1.3 Baseline  

## 1. Executive Summary
**AI-BOS** (AI Business Operating System) is an enterprise business operating system designed to solve owner dependency for SMBs and high-growth companies. It ensures that a business operates continuously, objectively, and accurately through a closed-loop, proof-based accountability and automatic work continuity engine — whether the owner is physically in the office or away.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
- **Proof-Gated Accountability**: Every task, including lead follow-ups, requires verifiable proof (documents, payment references, checklists) to earn completion credit. Zero credit is awarded without approved proof.
- **Automatic Work Continuity**: When an employee is on approved leave, AI-BOS automatically reassigns active tasks to pre-approved backups with the lowest current workload — eliminating silent task drop-offs.
- **Founder Intelligence Framework**: Captures explicit owner priority weightings and revenue goals to drive task priorities and exception escalations. Owner override decisions calibrate tenant AI sensitivity over time.
- **MVP Meaningful AI**: Features Proof Anomaly Flagging (flags duplicate/suspicious proof submissions for review) and Intelligent Daily Business Summary (narrative daily digest for the owner).
